চিঠি · The letter

What I'd say if I were braver.

Mithila,

I have started this a hundred times and thrown away ninety-nine. The problem was never that I had nothing to say — it's that everything true about you sounds too big when I write it down, and too small when I say it out loud.

So here is the plain version. Before you, my days were something to get through. Now they're something I look forward to, and the only thing that has changed is that you're in them. You made the ordinary hours worth keeping. You made me want to be a softer, steadier, better person — not because you asked me to, but because you were kind to me first and I didn't want to waste it.

I love the small things. The pause before you laugh. The way you defend the people you love. How you argue with me and still reach for my hand halfway through. How you make a room feel like somewhere I'm allowed to rest.

I don't know how to promise you the future, so I'll promise you the present instead: I will keep choosing you on the loud days and the quiet ones, on the days you feel like the moon and the days you feel like the rain. You have all of me. You have had it for a while now.

Come find me at the end of this page.

Himel
